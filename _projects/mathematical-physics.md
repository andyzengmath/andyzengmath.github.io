## Overview

Research at the intersection of differential geometry and mathematical physics, focusing on gauge theory, operator quantization, and their algebraic and geometric structures.

## Publications

### A Categorical Penrose-Ward Correspondence for Self-Dual Yang-Mills Theory

**In Preparation**

_Jonathan Block, Andy Zeng_

A dg refinement of the Penrose-Ward correspondence, relating self-dual Yang-Mills deformation theory to cohesive modules and derived twistor geometry.

---

### Affine Deformation and Global Symbols for the Marked Euler Groupoid

**In Preparation**

_Andy Zeng_

Affine deformation and global symbol calculus for the marked Euler groupoid, with operator-quantization constructions and a secondary algebraic/index application. This work does not claim a new Baum-Connes case or a general deformation $K$-invariance theorem.
