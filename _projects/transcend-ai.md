## About Transcend AI

**Transcend AI** is an AI-driven platform designed to enhance the efficiency and precision of decision-making for primary care pediatricians, with a focus on identifying potential rare genetic disease cases.

This project was part of the **Penn I-Corps** program at the Penn Center for Innovation.

## Key Insights

Our key lessons learned:

1. **Technology is one piece of the puzzle** — Technology and clinical decision-making tools represent a small segment within the broader healthcare delivery process
2. **Strategic entry points matter** — Identifying the right entry points for our technology is crucial in aligning our solutions with the key stages of care provision
