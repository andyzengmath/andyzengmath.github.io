## What is Quiver?

**Quiver** is an infinite canvas designed specifically for mathematical research. It provides a flexible, visual workspace where mathematicians can:

- 📐 **Organize ideas** — Arrange theorems, proofs, and concepts spatially
- 🔗 **Connect concepts** — Draw relationships between mathematical objects
- ✍️ **Write math beautifully** — Full LaTeX support for equations and formulas
- 🤝 **Collaborate** — Share your canvas and work together in real-time

## Why Quiver?

Traditional note-taking tools are linear. But mathematical thinking isn't — it's a web of interconnected ideas, dependencies, and relationships. Quiver embraces this by giving you an infinite 2D space to think and create.
