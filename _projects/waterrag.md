## Overview

**WaterRAG** is a multi-agent retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework designed for the wastewater treatment domain to support near-zero carbon emission goals.

General-purpose LLMs exhibit limitations in specialized domains, including insufficient professional knowledge and hallucination tendencies. WaterRAG addresses this gap by constructing a domain-specific knowledge base and multi-agent coordination system for complex problem-solving in environmental engineering.
