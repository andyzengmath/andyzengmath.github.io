## Overview

This research program develops the theory of $L_\infty$-algebroids and Lie $\infty$-groupoids as fundamental structures in higher differential geometry. These objects provide the correct framework for studying homotopy-coherent phenomena in geometry and mathematical physics.

## Publications

### Derived Lie ∞-Groupoids and Algebroids in Higher Differential Geometry

**PhD Dissertation, University of Pennsylvania (2021)**

_Andy Zeng_

We study various problems arising in higher geometry using derived Lie $\infty$-groupoids and algebroids. We construct homotopical algebras for derived Lie $\infty$-groupoids and algebroids and study their homotopy-coherent representations. We apply these tools to singular foliations and their characteristic classes, and prove an $A_\infty$ de Rham theorem and higher Riemann-Hilbert correspondence for foliated manifolds.

### Higher Riemann-Hilbert Correspondence and Descent for Regular Foliations

**arXiv 2025; revised September 2026**

_Andy Zeng_

For regular smooth foliations, an $A_\infty$ integration functor compares bounded finite-rank leafwise superconnections with locally constant fiber-cohomology rank to higher transport with the same regularity. It is quasi-fully faithful in general and a quasi-equivalence on compact manifolds, with sheafified morphism complexes on the transport side. No corresponding general equivalence for singular foliations or arbitrary $L_\infty$-algebroids is asserted.
