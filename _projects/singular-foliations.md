## Overview

Singular foliations generalize the classical notion of foliations by allowing the leaves to have varying dimensions. This research develops new tools for understanding their geometry, topology, and characteristic classes using methods from higher category theory and derived geometry.

## Publications

### Higher Monodromy Stacks for Locally Resolvable Singular Foliations

**In Preparation**

_Andy Zeng_

A higher monodromy stack built from finite local geometric resolutions, with Maurer-Cartan and van Kampen descent. This encodes higher leafwise transport rather than transverse holonomy.
