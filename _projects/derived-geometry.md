## Overview

This research applies derived and higher-categorical methods to complex and smooth geometry, including coherent sheaves, involutive structures, homotopy-theoretic generalizations of geometric structures, and flatness and factorization questions for smooth functions.

## Publications

### Superconnections, descent, and monodromy on transversely holomorphic foliations

**arXiv 2026**

_Andy Zeng_

This work models derived coherent sheaves on transversely holomorphic foliations using mixed de Rham-Dolbeault superconnections. It combines compact realization and bounded descent with monodromy of the coherent heart, criteria for descent to ordinary holonomy, and a derived description of holomorphic suspensions.
