## Overview

This research addresses the growing challenges of managing and understanding increasingly complex software systems. As codebases grow larger and more interconnected, developers face significant barriers to understanding code structure, dependencies, and behavior.

## The Problem

Modern software systems face escalating complexity through three core challenges:

- **Dependency Hell** — Hidden couplings, circular references, and compatibility conflicts that obscure ripple effects of changes
- **Obscured Structure** — Architectural ambiguity that slows navigation, increases onboarding costs, and stifles refactoring  
- **Limited Explainability** — Poor visibility into code's purpose and impact, leading to debugging delays and redundant code

These issues compound into tangible business risks: higher development costs, slower innovation, and mounting technical debt.

## Research Direction

Traditional methods like manual code reviews lack scalability, while LLMs struggle with structured reasoning and cross-file context due to token limits. This research explores intelligent approaches to transform opaque codebases into understandable, navigable structures—enabling precise impact analysis, dependency management, and automated quality enforcement.
