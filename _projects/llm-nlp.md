## Overview

Research exploring language models for natural language processing, with a focus on efficiency improvements, evaluation methods, and domain-specific semantic representations.

## Publications

### Domain-Adaptive Pretraining Enhances Water Treatment Semantic Representation for Large-Scale Structured Literature Mining

**arXiv 2026**

_Mudi Zhai, Ruihong Qiu, Qingyun Zeng, T. David Waite, Bing-Jie Ni, Haoran Duan_

WaterBERT adapts SciBERT to water-treatment literature through continued pretraining on approximately 2.97 billion tokens. Fine-tuned models support classification and structured information extraction, with applications to a corpus of 693,211 abstracts. WaterKERS combines the resulting knowledge graph with lexical and dense retrieval to improve literature search.

### Taming SQL Complexity: LLM-Based Equivalence Evaluation for Text-to-SQL

**ICML 2025 Workshop NewInML (Poster)**

_Andy Zeng, Simin Ma, Arash Niknafs, Ashish Basran, Carol Szabo_

The rise of Large Language Models has significantly advanced Text-to-SQL systems, yet evaluating the semantic equivalence of generated SQL remains a challenge. This paper explores using LLMs to assess both semantic and "weak" semantic equivalence, analyzing common patterns of SQL equivalence and inequivalence.
