"The Shape of Inner Space" is a fascinating journey into the geometry that underlies string theory, written by Shing-Tung Yau, a Fields Medalist and one of the world's leading geometers, along with Steve Nadis. The book focuses on Calabi-Yau manifolds—complex geometric shapes that potentialy form the six hidden dimensions required by string theory.

Yau describes his own work in proving the existence of these manifolds, a breakthrough that was essential for the development of string theory. The book explains how the shape of these hidden dimensions determines the physical laws of our universe. It is a masterful blend of biography, history, and cutting-edge science that illuminates the profound connection between abstract geometry and the physical world.
