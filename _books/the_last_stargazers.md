"The Last Stargazers" by Emily Levesque offers a captivating exploration into the world of modern astronomy through the eyes of the people who observe the cosmos. Levesque, an award-winning astronomer, blends scientific expertise with engaging storytelling to provide a behind-the-scenes look at the lives of contemporary stargazers.

The book shares the stories and experiences of astronomers and observatory employees, detailing their adventures to remote mountaintops and some of the most inaccessible parts of the globe in the name of science. It delves into the daily realities, challenges, and unique delights of working at cutting-edge observatories, including anecdotes about everything from scientific breakthroughs to unexpected mishaps and encounters with nature.
