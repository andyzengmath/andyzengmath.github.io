Liang Sicheng's seminal work, often titled "A Pictorial History of Chinese Architecture" or "Chinese Architecture: A Pictorial History," offers a concise yet thorough examination of the development of traditional Chinese architectural styles and structural systems. Widely regarded as the "Father of Modern Chinese Architecture," Liang Sicheng pioneered the scientific study of Chinese architectural history, and his book, originally written in English for a Western audience, became a landmark in the field.

The book's primary aim is to explain the structural systems and evolution of ancient Chinese architecture through extensive use of photographs, detailed drawings, and diagrams. These visuals, including 152 halftones and 94 diagrams, are considered a text in themselves, offering fundamental lessons on the anatomy of Chinese structures with both Chinese and English captions.
