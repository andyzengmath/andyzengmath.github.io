"The Three-Body Problem" by Liu Cixin is the first novel in the "Remembrance of Earth's Past" trilogy, a science fiction series exploring humanity's contact with an alien civilization.

The story begins during China's Cultural Revolution, where astrophysicist Ye Wenjie witnesses her father's brutal death and endures political persecution. Disillusioned with humanity, she is later recruited to a secret military base called Red Coast, which aims to make contact with extraterrestrial life. Ye secretly sends a message into space and receives a response from an alien race known as the Trisolarans, encouraging them to invade Earth, believing humanity cannot save itself.
